# CSMP IAS — Quiz Question Bank (300 Questions)

**Status: Live — seeded into the `quiz_questions` table and served by `/quiz`.** This is a flat reference file of 300 original multiple-choice questions for UPSC, UPPCS, and BPSC Prelims aspirants. Q1–Q200 were the original bank; Q201–Q300 were added later to widen the random-draw pool per exam. Keep this file in sync with the database when adding more questions.

## Sourcing & methodology

Every question here is original — none are copied verbatim from any paid test-series or PYQ compilation. The topic distribution and difficulty level were grounded in publicly available information about recent exam cycles and General Studies Paper I patterns:

- **UPSC CSE Prelims:** 2023 (28 May 2023), 2024 (16 June 2024), 2025 (25 May 2025), 2026 (24 May 2026, notified 14 Jan 2026)
- **UPPCS Prelims:** 2024 (22 Dec 2024), 2025 (12 Oct 2025)
- **BPSC Prelims:** 69th CCE (30 Sept 2023), 70th CCE Integrated (13 Dec 2024, re-exam 4 Jan 2025 at one centre), 71st CCE (13 Sept 2025), 72nd CCE (tentatively 26 July 2026)

All three exams share a common GS Paper I backbone — History & Culture, Indian & World Geography, Polity & Governance, Economic & Social Development, Environment & Ecology, General Science, and Current Affairs — with UPPCS and BPSC each adding a compulsory state-specific GK component (Uttar Pradesh / Bihar respectively). Recent cycles (2023–2025) leaned heavily on: government schemes (PM-Kisan, Ayushman Bharat, PM Vishwakarma, PM Surya Ghar), constitutional/polity developments (Women's Reservation Act, Article 370 abrogation, GST Council), India's G20 presidency and BRICS expansion, ISRO missions (Chandrayaan-3, Aditya-L1, XPoSat, Gaganyaan, NISAR), Union Budget/Economic Survey themes, and COP28/29 environment outcomes — all reflected in the Current Affairs subsections below.
